Wide illustration of one scene from a classical heroic novel, for a mobile story game. Clear readable composition, figures large enough to recognize, calm areas of plain paper around the figures.

Scene: A crowded market street in late-Joseon Seoul in front of a tobacco shop: a jeongisu storyteller (man in a black gat and grey dopo) sits on a low stool reading an open thread-bound novel aloud with dramatic gestures, a circle of commoners, children and a peddler listen spellbound, some tossing copper coins onto a straw mat.

Art style: hand-colored Korean woodblock print, like the illustrations of late-Joseon commercially printed classical novels (bangakbon) and the Oryun Haengsildo: bold carved black outlines with the slight irregularity of a carved wood block, flat areas of hand-applied color limited to vermilion red, indigo blue, ochre yellow and soft green, visible wood grain and ink-press texture, cream mulberry paper background. Strong graphic readability, printed look, not a painting. Absolutely no text, no letters, no calligraphy, no seals, no signatures, no captions, no frames or borders. This is late-Joseon Korea (18th-19th century): people wear Korean hanbok, men wear black horsehair gat hats or headbands.
