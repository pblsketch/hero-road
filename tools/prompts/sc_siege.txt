Wide illustration of one scene from a classical heroic novel, for a mobile story game. Clear readable composition, figures large enough to recognize, calm areas of plain paper around the figures.

Scene: The climax of a war tale: the emperor in a yellow dragon robe is surrounded by enemy soldiers on a battlefield, about to surrender, when a young male hero about 17, son of a loyal noble family, bright determined eyes, straight black eyebrows, hair in a topknot, dark-gold scale armor over a crimson robe, helmet with a red tassel charges in on a flying dragon-horse wreathed in clouds, sword raised, enemy soldiers falling back in terror; a fierce northern barbarian general, broad face, thick moustache, fur-trimmed helmet, dark lamellar armor with a wolf-fur mantle recoils.

Art style: hand-colored Korean woodblock print, like the illustrations of late-Joseon commercially printed classical novels (bangakbon) and the Oryun Haengsildo: bold carved black outlines with the slight irregularity of a carved wood block, flat areas of hand-applied color limited to vermilion red, indigo blue, ochre yellow and soft green, visible wood grain and ink-press texture, cream mulberry paper background. Strong graphic readability, printed look, not a painting. Absolutely no text, no letters, no calligraphy, no seals, no signatures, no captions, no frames or borders. The story world is an idealized Ming-dynasty China as imagined in late-Joseon Korean heroic novels, so every story character wears MING DYNASTY Chinese clothing and armor, never Korean hanbok.
